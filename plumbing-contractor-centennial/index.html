Discussing the work and its scope

A plumbing conversation should establish what is affected, what result you’re looking for, and what work is being proposed. For a repair, explain the symptoms and when they occur. For a planned change, identify the fixture or location and any access considerations. Ask what the proposed work includes, what it doesn’t include, and whether there are decisions you need to make before work begins.

Use this checklist before you call:

  • Identify the fixture and its location in the home.
